Common Chord Progressions

  • G - C - D: Simple and upbeat.
  • C - G - Am - F: Popular pop-style movement.
  • Em - C - G - D: Good for emotional or dramatic songs.
  • Am - C - G - D: Great for darker beginner practice.

Beginner Notes

  • Press just behind the fret wire, not directly on top of it.
  • Keep your fingers curved so they do not mute nearby strings.
  • Practice chord switches slowly before trying to play fast.
